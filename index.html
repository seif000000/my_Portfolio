I'm Seif Nady, a Computer Science student at Helwan University working across Full Stack Development and Workflow Automation. My work tends to live at the seam between the two: a real product with real users, and the automations that keep it running without manual babysitting.

Graduation Project

Your surplus medicine is someone else's lifeline. A bilingual platform connecting people with surplus or near-expiry medicine to the patients who need it — with an emergency board, verified pharmacies and AI that reads a medicine box straight from a photo, so nobody types Arabic drug names on a phone keyboard.

33Pages
